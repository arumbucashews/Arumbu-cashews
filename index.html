    The Founder

    Rooted in Agriculture. Built on Trust.

    The story behind Arumbu Cashews begins with hard work, agriculture and a commitment to doing things the right way.

    Sivakumar L

    Founder, Arumbu Cashews Farmer-Entrepreneur · Cashew & Jackfruit Cultivation

    Sivakumar L comes from a farming family and has spent his life working the land — cultivating a range of crops, with jackfruit and cashew as his main areas of focus. Years of hands-on agricultural experience shaped the way he thinks about quality: nothing beats produce that's grown, harvested and handled with genuine care.

    His approach has always been simple — work hard, stay genuine, maintain transparency, and give customers exactly what the product actually is, without unnecessary claims or exaggeration. That same mindset became the foundation Arumbu Cashews was built on.

    The name Arumbu carries personal meaning. It is the name of his mother, Arumbu, and it now carries her name forward as the identity of the brand. His father, Late Lakshmanaperumal, was an important part of the family's agricultural journey — and his legacy continues to shape the values behind the business.

    Arumbu Cashews carries that legacy forward — rooted in family, grounded in agriculture, and built on the same honesty Sivakumar has practiced his entire working life.

    Our Story

    A family name, a standard to live up to

    Arumbu started with a simple frustration — most "premium" cashews on shelves were anything but fresh. We set out to shorten the distance between farm and family, working directly with growers across Tamil Nadu who share our obsession with quality.

    Every sack that reaches our sorting floor is inspected by hand before it earns the Arumbu name. It's slower than buying in bulk from traders — but it's the only way we know how to do this properly.
